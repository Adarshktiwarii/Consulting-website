Method

How engagements run

The same three-stage method underlies both tracks — Track 02 adds a coordination layer on top.

01 / DISCOVER & DEFINE

Understand before building

Deep-dive into your product, users, and market. Identify the real problems worth solving and build a prioritized roadmap with clear milestones.

02 / BUILD & SHIP

Execute with discipline

Work with engineering, design, and — where hardware is involved — device teams to ship quickly, with clear specs and relentless focus on what moves the needle.

03 / MEASURE & SCALE

Prove it, then grow it

Every initiative gets measured. Set up analytics, run experiments, and iterate based on data for sustainable growth.

On Track 02 — a fourth layer runs underneath all three: weekly team syncs, client reporting, and ownership of blockers, so you get one accountable lead instead of a set of independent contributors.

Common questions

Before you reach out

The things most founders ask before the first call.

Do you only work with health-tech companies? Yes — it's the one domain I go deep in, so every engagement benefits from pattern-matching across FHIR/HL7, HIPAA, and clinical workflows I already know.
Can you work with pre-seed or very early startups? Yes — Track 01 is built for exactly this: a scoped, fast engagement to validate direction before you commit to a bigger build.
Do you sign NDAs? Standard practice — happy to sign yours, or share mine, before any detailed discussion.
What does a typical Track 02 engagement look like? A quarter or more, weekly cadence, me plus 1–2 specialists I bring in — you get one accountable lead instead of managing several contractors.
What does this actually cost? Scoped per project rather than a fixed rate card — Track 01 and Track 02 land in very different ranges depending on depth. I'll give you a clear number within a day of a scoping call, no guessing games.
What if it's not a fit after we start? Either of us can call it early. Track 01 is scoped in short, clean checkpoints; Track 02 has a defined trial period before the longer commitment kicks in — no long lock-in either way.
Do you work with bootstrapped teams, or only funded ones? Both — the track matters more than the funding stage. Bootstrapped teams tend to fit Track 01; funded teams with a bigger initiative often need Track 02's coordination layer.
Who owns the work product? You do. Strategy docs, code, and deliverables are yours on completion — standard practice, spelled out in the engagement agreement before we start.
